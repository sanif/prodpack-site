# Closed Beta Refund Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

A paid-beta purchaser may request a refund within 14 calendar days after the
purchase date by emailing <support@prodpack.dev> from the purchase email and
stating the reason for the request.

ProdPack will also refund the purchase if it cannot provide a signed macOS build
that launches on a documented supported system, or if a reproduced blocker in a
verified golden path cannot be resolved or reasonably worked around during the
14-day period.

After 14 days, refunds are not normally provided for:

- a change of mind;
- missing custom features that were not promised in writing;
- third-party account, provider, cloud, DNS, or credential problems;
- incompatibility introduced by changes to a generated repository;
- packs labeled Composition checked or External verification required in
  `PACK_MATURITY.md`; or
- failure to meet a buyer-specific legal, regulatory, accessibility, or tax
  requirement that was not agreed in writing.

Mandatory consumer rights in the purchaser's jurisdiction continue to apply and
take priority over this policy.

When a refund is issued, the purchaser's license to use ProdPack ends and the
purchaser must delete the application and private release files. Rights in
repositories generated while the license was valid remain governed by
`GENERATED_OUTPUT_TERMS.md`.

Refunds are returned through the original payment method when possible. Payment
provider processing time may apply.

This policy should be reviewed against the actual checkout provider and sales
jurisdictions before accepting paid orders.
