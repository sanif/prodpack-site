# ProdPack Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

## Summary

The ProdPack desktop application is local-first. It does not require a ProdPack
account, send project source code to ProdPack, or include ProdPack analytics or
advertising SDKs. Generation happens on the buyer's Mac.

## Desktop application data

ProdPack stores settings, recent-project paths, and generation metadata locally
on the buyer's Mac. Generated source files are written only to the location the
buyer selects.

ProdPack does not intentionally collect:

- generated source code;
- project names or paths;
- environment values or production secrets;
- credentials for third-party services;
- command output from local bootstrap tools; or
- usage telemetry from the desktop application.

When a buyer asks ProdPack to install dependencies, start containers, open a
link, or use a provider tool, that tool may communicate with its own service.
For example, Bun, Python package indexes, Docker registries, GitHub, AWS, Stripe,
or Supabase apply their own privacy policies. Those communications do not pass
through a ProdPack-operated proxy.

## Website, purchase, and support data

The static website host may process standard request data such as IP address,
user agent, requested URL, and timestamp. Hosted fonts, icon CDNs, or other
third-party assets may receive the same request data until those assets are
inlined.

For a paid beta invitation, ProdPack may receive the purchaser's name, email,
company, order details, support correspondence, and payment status. Payment is
processed by the provider identified at checkout or on the invoice. ProdPack
does not receive or store full payment-card numbers.

## Purposes

Purchase and support data is used to:

- provide the signed download and eligible updates;
- administer seats and the beta relationship;
- respond to support and security reports;
- issue invoices, refunds, and legally required records; and
- improve the product using feedback the buyer chooses to provide.

ProdPack does not sell personal data or use it for third-party behavioral
advertising.

## Retention

Order and tax records are retained for the period required by applicable law.
Support and beta correspondence is retained while useful for the relationship,
security, or dispute handling, then deleted or anonymized. Local application
data remains under the buyer's control and can be removed by deleting ProdPack's
application data and generated folders.

## Sharing

Data may be shared with service providers used for hosting, email, payment,
invoicing, crash investigation requested by the buyer, and legal compliance.
Providers receive only the information reasonably needed for their service.
Data may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or
as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

## Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used for purchase and
support data. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Buyers
must not email production secrets or private keys in a support request. A
minimal reproduction with secrets removed is preferred.

## Buyer choices

Subject to applicable law, a person may request access, correction, deletion,
or a copy of personal data by emailing <support@prodpack.dev>. Identity may need
to be verified before a request is completed.

## Children

ProdPack is a professional developer tool and is not directed to children.

## Changes

Material changes will be dated and provided to active beta buyers. Changes do
not reduce rights in already generated repositories.

Privacy questions may be sent to <support@prodpack.dev>.

This policy should be reviewed for the seller's actual hosting, payment, email,
and tax providers before accepting paid orders.
