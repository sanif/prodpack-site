Questions, answered

Do I own the code?

You own your project-specific code and receive broad, perpetual commercial rights to reusable generated ProdPack code. Third-party packages keep their own licenses. Generated projects have no ProdPack runtime, royalty, account, or license-server dependency. Read the generated-output terms before ordering.

What counts as a seat?

A seat is one developer authorized to run the ProdPack app. Your beta invitation and order confirmation state the seat count. Client and commercial output rights do not depend on the pilot profile shown above.

Do I get updates?

Every paid-beta purchase includes verified updates released during the 12 months after purchase. You may keep using the last eligible app version afterward. Later updates or support may require an optional new purchase or renewal. Generated projects keep working independently.

Is there a refund?

Eligibility depends on the failure and the law that applies to your order. Review the refund policy before paying, then contact support with the required details if a documented supported-system or verified golden-path failure qualifies.

What happens before payment?

We confirm fit, price, seller identity, seats, supported systems, policy links, and the 12-month update window in writing. There is no public self-serve checkout or download during the closed beta.

Test a verified golden path

Tell us what you build, which auth path you need, and how many people would use the app. We will reply with fit and pilot terms before requesting payment.
